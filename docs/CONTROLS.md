# CONTROLS.md

The full list also lives in-game: **Pause → Controls**. Gamepad works everywhere (Xbox-style names below); menus navigate with arrows/stick + Enter/A, back with Esc/B.

## Moving

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Run | WASD / Arrow keys | Left stick (analog speed) |
| Jump / Double jump | Space | A |
| Tail Spin | J or X | X |
| Stomp (in the air) | K or B | B |
| Chomp / carry / spit | L or C | Y |
| Mega Roar | J with a FULL Brain Power meter | X |
| Talk / look / use | E or Enter | RB |
| Ask Digger for a hint | H | LB |

## Camera

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Orbit | Q / O (or drag with the mouse) | Right stick |
| Zoom steps | V (or mouse wheel) | L3 |
| Recentre behind Max | R | R3 |
| Invert / sensitivity | Settings → Camera | — |

## Menus & extras

- **Pause:** Esc or P (Start).
- **Skip cutscene:** Esc or the Skip button.
- **Answering questions:** stomp or stand on an answer podium in the world, or click/choose the button in panels. Every question can be re-read aloud with the 🔊 button.
- **Explorer or Hero** difficulty can be changed any time in Settings → Adventure style.

## Movement tips (from Marcus's tutorial)

- You keep your double jump even after walking off a ledge — and there's a moment of *coyote time* right after the edge where a jump still counts.
- Press jump slightly before landing and Max jumps the instant he touches down.
- Release jump early for a small hop, hold it for full height.
- Stomp breaks **cracked plates** — Digger sniffs near buried secrets.
- Bounce pads launch higher once the Spring Boots are built at Kenji's Workshop.
- Chomp a small Cogling Scout… then spit it at something. Marcus insists this is honourable.
