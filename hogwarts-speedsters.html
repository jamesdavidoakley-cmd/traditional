Full 3D! Pick a magical creature. Charge your power. Go FAST through the castle! 🏰
How to play: Your creature sprints forward on its own — you steer and jump to dodge! Grab ⚡ galleons to fill your power bar. When it's full a giant golden message tells you exactly which key to press. Smash into a bad guy with a full bar and your power fires by itself! 💥
